# FAQ

Short answers; link to the other kit pages for detail.

**Is this real patient data?**
No. Every claim, payer, rate and dollar is made up. No real patients, payers or money.

**Can I see the code or the repo?**
Not at this time: the repository is private. What is shared: every prompt Erum gave
([prompts.md](prompts.md)), how it was built and checked ([method.md](method.md)) and the tools
([tools.md](tools.md)).

**How long would this take a normal team?**
It depends on the team, the data and the organization, so I don't compare. My own numbers for this
build, with what they include, are in [method.md](method.md#what-it-took-me). The videos show the cycle
(ask, see it, review, change it) and what a real delivery still needs people for.

**Which AI did the work?**
Claude Code built it; Codex reviewed it independently. Erum directed every phase and approved every gate.

**Did the AI make mistakes?**
Yes, and they are logged. Each video shows one, in the segment "The catch", with how it was caught.

**Does this mean you don't need a team?**
No. The series shows what the tool can do, not that people can be removed. Someone still sets the intent, decides the trade-offs and signs off. The AI
speeds up execution and produces the evidence. A real delivery still needs subject matter experts to
confirm the rules, end users to confirm it fits their work, and engineers for production.

**Would this work in production or a regulated environment?**
Not shown. The build stops at a dry run. UAT, a QA environment, production and validation paperwork were
out of scope.

**Is the channel sponsored?**
No. Tools are named because they were used.

**Is the narrator a real person?**
No. "Helena" is an AI voice, and the b-roll is AI-generated. Both are disclosed in every description.

**Can you build this for my company?**
Not at the moment. This is a side exploration, and everything learned is shared here for free.
