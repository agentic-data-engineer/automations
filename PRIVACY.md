# Privacy policy: ADE comments

Last updated October 6, 2026. Written against the YouTube API Services Developer Policies and the Google
API Services User Data Policy.

ADE comments is a small private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Agentic Data Engineer,
to manage that channel. It uses the **YouTube API Services**. By interacting with the channel through it
(for example, receiving a reply to your comment), you are also covered by the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and Google's handling of data is described in
the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What it accesses

With the channel owner's sign-in, the tool:

- reads public comments on the channel's videos: the comment text, the commenter's public display name,
  the date, and which video it is on;
- posts replies that the channel owner has approved one by one;
- at the owner's direction, updates the channel's own video details (title, description, tags,
  playlists, schedule), the channel description and banner, and removes the owner's own videos.

It does not access commenters' accounts, private messages, email addresses or any other data.

## How the data is used and shared

Comment data is used only to help the channel owner read and answer comments. It is kept in the owner's
private workspace: on the owner's own computer, and in a private dashboard on claude.ai where Anthropic's
Claude, an AI assistant the owner uses, sorts comments and drafts replies for the owner to approve. That
is the only third party that processes it, and only to provide this feature. Comment data is not sold,
not used for advertising, not shared with anyone else, and not used to train AI models.

## How long it is kept

Comment data the tool stores is deleted within 30 days of being collected. Public comments themselves
stay on YouTube, under YouTube's terms.

## Cookies

The tool places no cookies or similar technology on anyone's device or browser.

## Your choices

- To remove your comment, delete it on YouTube.
- To ask for any stored copy of your comment to be deleted, open a discussion in this repository; it is
  deleted within 7 days.
- The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at
  [Google's security settings](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
